Cancellations & Rescheduling

DRAFT — these policy pages are starter drafts for review. They are not legal advice and must be reviewed by Nicola before publication.

We understand that plans can change. To help us manage our time and reserve your booking slot, we ask that parents and carers give clear notice if a session needs to be cancelled or rescheduled.

1. Cancellation & Rescheduling Notice

All cancellations or rescheduling must be notified at least 24 hours in advance of the booked session. Notice is given by email or text message to 07368 512670 or info@thereadingbridgesw.co.uk.

2. Late Cancellation or Non-Attendance

If a session is cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or if a booked session is not attended ("no-show"), the full fee for that session will be due. This policy allows us to reserve the time for your child even when it cannot be used.

3. Our Cancellations

If we need to cancel or reschedule a session on our side, we will give you as much notice as possible and will offer a replacement session or a full refund, at your discretion.

4. Illness

If either you or your child is unwell, please let us know as soon as possible. Sessions may be rescheduled in line with the notice policy above.
