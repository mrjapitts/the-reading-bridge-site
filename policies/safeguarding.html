Safeguarding

DRAFT — these policy pages are starter drafts for review. They are not legal advice and must be reviewed by Nicola before publication.

The safety, welfare and wellbeing of every child is our top priority. The Reading Bridge operates a safeguarding policy in line with the guidance issued by the Department for Education, to ensure that whenever we are in contact with children and young people we take reasonable steps to keep them safe.

1. Our Responsibilities

  • To safeguard and promote the welfare of the children we work with;
  • To ensure all practice and procedures reflect statutory and governmental guidance;
  • To make sure that all staff and anyone working on behalf of The Reading Bridge are aware of their responsibilities and follow safe working practices.

2. Safer Recruitment & DBS

All tutors working with children hold a current and valid Enhanced Disclosure from the Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS). This disclosure is checked and copies are retained on the personnel file in line with statutory requirements.

3. Recognising and Responding to Abuse

We will respond to concerns about a child's welfare in line with our safeguarding procedures. If we have any concerns about a child's safety or welfare we will act promptly — this includes speaking with the child's parents/carers where appropriate, and, where necessary, referring to the relevant local authority children's social care services.

4. Confidentiality

Information about a child or family is treated as confidential. It is only shared with authorised members of staff on a need-to-know basis and will not be passed to third parties without the consent of the parents/carers, except where we are under a legal or professional duty to disclose, or where it is necessary to protect the welfare of a child.

5. Contact

For questions about our safeguarding arrangements, or if you have a concern, please contact us at info@thereadingbridgesw.co.uk.
