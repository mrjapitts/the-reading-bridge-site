Privacy & GDPR

DRAFT — these policy pages are starter drafts for review. They are not legal advice and must be reviewed by Nicola before publication.

The Reading Bridge ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting and preserving the privacy of parents, carers and the children we serve. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used, stored and shared.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the personal data necessary to provide tuition and to meet our legal safeguarding obligations. This includes:

  • Name(s) and contact details of parent/guardian and child (including email and telephone number).
  • Information about the child's learning needs, assessment results and progress, where provided by you or a qualified assessor.
  • Correspondence records relating to enquiries, bookings and sessions.

We do not require, nor do we collect, sensitive special-category data unless you voluntarily provide it and it is relevant to supporting the child.

2. How We Use Your Data

Personal data is used solely to:

  • Arrange and deliver teaching and learning sessions;
  • Communicate about sessions, progress and any necessary adjustments;
  • Fulfil our safeguarding and duty-of-care responsibilities;
  • Keep records required by law, including any records relevant to working with children and vulnerable adults.

3. Legal Basis & Storage

We process personal data under the lawful bases of contract (where a service agreement exists), legal obligation (safeguarding) and, where consent is relied upon, freely given, specific and informed consent. Data is stored securely, kept only for as long as necessary, and is not transferred outside the United Kingdom.

4. Data Retention

Records are retained only for the period required to fulfil the purposes for which they were collected, or as required by law. Where a family's no longer in session, personal data is retained only as long as legally required and then securely destroyed.

5. Your Rights

Under data protection law you have the right of access to your personal data, the right to rectify inaccurate data, the right to request erasure, the right to restrict processing, and the right to object to processing. You may exercise these rights by contacting us at info@thereadingbridgesw.co.uk.

6. Complaints

If you have concerns about how your data has been handled, please contact us in the first instance. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office.
