The Assessment

The Assessment Day

This Is Not a 'Pass or Fail' Test. Please reassure your child that they cannot 'fail' this session. This assessment is simply a puzzle that helps me map out how their brain processes language.

Your child cannot "pass" or "fail" this session — it is simply a puzzle that helps us map how their brain processes language.

45 to 60 minutes

Because it is conducted 1-to-1 in a quiet, relaxed space, it feels much more like an informal quiz or a game than a rigid school exam.

Child-led

If your child needs a brain break, a stretch, or a glass of water, we pause immediately. They are entirely in control.

Recognised Assessment Tools Used

  • The WRAT5Word lists, quick spelling games and short tasks — no long, intimidating stories.
  • The Access Reading TestShort passages read silently, answered as multi-choice puzzles.
  • The YARCYour child reads a short, specially levelled story aloud.
The three named instruments (WRAT5, Access Reading Test, YARC) are examples of recognised tools that may be used. ⚠️ TO CONFIRM — confirm these are the instruments currently used before publishing.

Preparing Your Child

  • Keep It Low-KeyA calm, relaxed morning beats a big production.
  • Rest and FuelA good night's sleep and a protein-rich snack make a real difference.
  • What to BringPrescription reading glasses, a coloured overlay or a tracking ruler if your child uses these.

The diagnostics are scored and a Diagnostic Baseline Profile is compiled within ~48 hours, followed by a short follow-up call to walk you through what it all means.
