Payment Terms

DRAFT — these policy pages are starter drafts for review. They are not legal advice and must be reviewed by Nicola before publication.

We aim to make our payment arrangements simple and transparent. This Payment Terms policy sets out how fees are calculated, when payment is due and the methods available to you.

1. Fees

Details of our session fees are set out in our pricing information and on request. Fees may be reviewed from time to time; you will be given reasonable notice before any change to fees takes effect.

2. Invoicing & Payment Schedule

Invoices are issued in advance of booked sessions unless otherwise agreed in writing. Payment is expected in line with the terms agreed at the start of each period of tuition.

3. Payment Methods

We accept payment by bank transfer. Full payment details are provided on request or on your invoice.

4. Late or Non-Payment

If payment is not received by the due date, we will contact you to arrange settlement. Sessions may be paused until outstanding balances are settled, at our discretion.

For any questions about fees or payment, please contact us at info@thereadingbridgesw.co.uk.
