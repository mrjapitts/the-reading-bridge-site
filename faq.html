FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents.

What ages and key stages do you work with?
⚠️ TO CONFIRM — artwork is inconsistent (KS2–KS3 vs KS2–KS4). Working with KS2, KS3 and KS4 learners, including the 9–14 age range for the phonics catch-up track.
Do you have SEND and dyslexia experience?
Yes. Nicola is a specialist in Enhanced Learning Provision and designs all support for neurodiverse learners, dyslexic profiles and home-educated children.
Do you work online or face-to-face?
1:1 tutoring is delivered face-to-face. Bridge Learning Pods are small-group sessions. Tell us your preference when you book and we'll advise what suits your child.
How long is a session?
Typically 45 to 60 minutes, conducted 1-to-1 in a quiet, relaxed space.
What happens at the assessment?
It is not a pass or fail test — your child cannot fail. It feels more like an informal quiz or a game, and your child is entirely in control, including taking breaks. A Diagnostic Baseline Profile is then compiled within ~48 hours, followed by a short follow-up call.
How quickly will I get results?
The Diagnostic Baseline Profile is usually compiled within about 48 hours, followed by a short follow-up call to walk you through it.
Do you set homework?
⚠️ TO CONFIRM — confirm whether any between-session practice is set, and if so what. Answer: to be confirmed.
What if my child is anxious?
This is very common and completely understood. Sessions are kept calm and child-led, with breaks whenever needed. Your child is entirely in control.
Which area do you cover?
Somerset and Devon.
